# libSceAgcDriver

## add Functional

- [Mip levels past the full chain](../../../core/libs/prx/libSceAgcDriver/Graphics/src/TextureTiling.cpp) (libSceAgcDriver) - a T# or color target may name more mip levels than floor(log2(max(width, height, depth))) + 1; Demon's Souls (PPSA01342) samples 28x1 and 4x1 BC3 and BC7 arrays with 8 to 11 levels. On RDNA a level's extent is max(1, extent >> level), so every level past that count is one more 1x1 level with texels of its own in guest memory. A Vulkan image cannot have those levels (`VUID-VkImageCreateInfo-mipLevels-00947`), so sampled textures, their block and depth copies and storage images hold the levels of the full chain only (`SurfaceGeometry::imageLevels`): the levels past it are neither uploaded nor written back, and their guest bytes keep their values. Filtered samples and gathers through a view whose base level, last level or minimum LOD lies past the chain read the image's last level instead of their texels (the other shader accesses of such a view are listed under T#s whose levels reach past the full mip chain in [shader](shader.md)); whether the console's LOD selection (`BASE_LEVEL`, `LAST_LEVEL`, `MIN_LOD`, the sampler's LOD clamp) reaches such a level, and what it returns there, is not verified on hardware. A storage image access or a color target at such a level throws. A fill or a buffer copy over a storage surface with such levels is stored to guest memory instead of becoming a clear or an image copy (`StorageTexture::FillClear`, `StorageTexture::CopyFrom`), and such a surface is tracked per layer, not per 64 KiB block. A DCC clear code over it clears the levels of the chain only: once its results are stored, the keys read as uncompressed and the levels past the chain keep their earlier bytes.

# shader

## add Functional

- [T#s whose levels reach past the full mip chain](../../../core/shader/recompiler/Optimization/src/ResourceMaterializer.cpp) - the driver's view of such a T# holds only the levels of its extent's full chain (Mip levels past the full chain in [libSceAgcDriver](libSceAgcDriver.md)). `image_get_resinfo` returns the decoded view's level count and 1 for the spatial extents at a level past the chain but inside that count; for the tested descriptors with `MAX_MIP = LAST_LEVEL`, the count is `LAST_LEVEL - BASE_LEVEL + 1`, as `query_levels` and `lower_query_size` in Mesa's `src/amd/common/nir/ac_nir_lower_resinfo.c` derive them from the descriptor; the levels the view lacks reach the shader as a pipeline specialization constant, or in bits 22-25 of the runtime image metadata flags of a registration module. MIMG BY2/BY4 and PCK2/PCK4 loads at a level the view lacks return zero, as the hardware does at every level narrower than their N texels, and every level past the chain is 1x1. `image_load_mip`, `image_get_lod`, `image_load` through a T# whose base level lies past the chain, and `image_get_resinfo` through an image table whose view lacks levels are refused (the draw or dispatch throws, an image table entry faults): the texels of the levels past the chain are not represented, and an image table does not carry the levels the view lacks. At a LOD at or past that count, width, height and depth or layer count are zero and the level count is retained. The tested query values and distinct texels at extra mip-load addresses agree with [externally reported RX 6700 XT (gfx1031) HIP measurements](https://github.com/boykopovar/AnyPS5/pull/6280#issuecomment-6254884569); these are desktop RDNA2 measurements, not PS5 measurements, and do not establish sampling or gather behavior past the chain. The existing decoder clamps raw `LAST_LEVEL` to `MAX_MIP` when `BASE_LEVEL <= MAX_MIP`; that behavior is unchanged here. The externally reported 28x1 2D descriptor with `LAST_LEVEL=9` and `MAX_MIP=0` returns ten levels, which the existing decode clamp does not reproduce. That discrepancy is outside the tested boundary fix; a CPU control with last level 9 and maximum mip 6 preserves the current decode behavior.
